What We Do

02 / Mandate
i.

Convene

Meetings, conferences, conclaves and roundtables held time to time, bringing academic and industry leaders together to read the sector's opportunities and challenges.

ii.

Disseminate

Findings and proceedings are published openly on our website, for the benefit of the wider academic community.

iii.

Advise Regulators

Where our discussions surface a clear opportunity or concern, we offer considered suggestions to the regulators who govern the sector.

How We Work

03 / Method
Convenings

Closed-door roundtables

Senior academic and industry leaders speak candidly on the issues shaping higher education.

Publications

Open briefs & proceedings

Each convening is translated into a plain record, published for any institution to read and use.

Advisory

Non-partisan recommendations

Shared with regulators when a discussion points to a clear, actionable opportunity.
